Restore a subscription

On the subscription screen tap Restore Purchases, or in the app open Settings (the gear on Home) → Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

Cancel or change your plan

In the app: Settings → Manage Subscription. Or on your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → YoMilly. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

Your lyrics

Your songs sync through your own iCloud account and are there on every iPhone you sign into. To keep a copy anywhere else, tap the download icon at the top of the subscription screen and save them as text files — with or without a subscription.

Refunds

Payments are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
