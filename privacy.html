Privacy Policy

YoMilly · last updated 19 September 2026

YoMilly is an app for writing rap. This page says exactly what happens to what you write, in plain language. There is no tracking, no advertising, and nothing is sold to anybody.

Your songs stay yours

Everything you write — songs, bars, notes, saved lyrics, your name and your picture — is kept on your device and synced through your own private iCloud account. It goes from your phone to Apple's servers and back to your other devices. We cannot read it, cannot list it, and have no way to reach it. If you sign out of iCloud, it stays on the device.

AI features

Some features — Milly, Ask the Rappers, line checks, word pages and your stats — send the line or lines you are asking about to our server, which uses AI services, including OpenAI, to answer. The app asks your permission before it sends anything, and you can turn this off at any time in the pad's Settings under AI Features.

  • Only the lines needed to answer are sent. Not your whole song, not your name, not your account.
  • Those lines are not stored by us.
  • No identifier is attached, so requests cannot be tied to a person or to each other.
  • OpenAI states that data sent through its API is not used to train its models.

Your voice

When you record a freestyle or dictate a bar, the speech is turned into text on your phone, by Apple's on-device speech recognition. The audio is never uploaded, never stored, and never reaches us. What you choose to keep afterwards is saved like any other lyric — on your device and in your iCloud.

Photos and beats

A cover photo you choose, or a beat you import, is copied into the app and stays there and in your iCloud. Neither is uploaded to us.

Subscriptions

Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your card, your Apple Account, or your name.

We use RevenueCat to check whether a subscription is active. RevenueCat receives a random identifier generated for your install, the receipt Apple issues, and your country and device type. It does not receive anything you have written. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Artist pictures

Pictures of artists are fetched from Deezer's public catalogue by our server, not by your phone. Deezer is not told anything about you.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, no advertising, no third-party trackers.
  • No accounts, no sign-up, no email address required.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone for marketing.

Children

YoMilly is not aimed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Deleting everything

Delete the app to remove everything on the device. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → YoMilly → Delete. Because we hold nothing of yours, there is nothing on our side to delete — but you can write to us and we will confirm that.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it and the new version appears here.
